Gas turbine control benchmark: live demo

A power-plant gas turbine must spin at exactly its rated speed (60 Hz) while the electrical load changes. Four controllers try to hold it. Change the load, or make the turbine different from what the controllers expect, and watch what happens. Everything runs in your browser. Code, method and full results on GitHub.
